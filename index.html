01Overview

Anglers ("the app", "we", "us") is a mobile puzzle game developed and published by an independent developer operating under the name Bwana. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and who it's shared with when you play Anglers.

Anglers does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide your name, address, or any contact details to play. There is no cloud save and no cross-device sync — your progress lives only on your device.

02Information we collect

Anglers itself does not directly ask you for personal information. Some information is collected automatically by the third-party services built into the app, as described below.

Data collected by service
ServiceWhat it collectsPurpose
Analytics
(e.g. Firebase Analytics / GameAnalytics)		 Gameplay events (levels played, completion, retries), session length, device model, OS version, language, approximate location (country/region derived from IP), and crash/diagnostic logs. Understand how the game is played, find and fix bugs, and balance level difficulty.
Advertising
(Google AdMob)		 Advertising ID, device information, IP-derived approximate location, and ad interaction data (impressions, clicks). Show ads that support the free game, and measure ad performance. Where privacy law requires it, you're asked for consent and can change it at any time (see Your choices).
In-app purchases
(Google Play Billing)		 Purchase confirmation and transaction ID. Payment details (card numbers, billing address) are handled entirely by Google Play and are never seen by us. Process and deliver purchased in-game items.

We don't collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise GPS location, contacts, photos, or any account credentials — because the app never asks for them.

03How we use information

  • To operate and improve Anglers — diagnosing crashes, fixing bugs, and adjusting level difficulty based on aggregate play data.
  • To display and measure advertising that keeps the app free to play.
  • To process optional in-app purchases through Google Play Billing.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it to build a personal profile of you outside of what the analytics and advertising services above do on our behalf.

04How information is shared

We don't operate our own servers that store player data. Information described above is shared only with the third-party providers that collect it directly on our behalf, currently:

  • Google (AdMob, Firebase, Google Play Billing) — Google Privacy Policy

If Anglers adds another analytics or advertising provider in the future, this policy will be updated to name it before the change takes effect.

05Your choices

  • In-game privacy choices: where privacy law requires it (for example the EEA, UK, or certain US states), Anglers shows a consent form the first time you play, and a Privacy choices option stays available in Settings afterward so you can review or change your ad consent at any time. If your region doesn't require this, the option won't appear, since it isn't legally needed there.
  • Device-level control: on Android, go to Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Google → Ads, depending on your device) and turn on "Opt out of Ads Personalization" or reset your advertising ID. This works regardless of region.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored game data from your device, since nothing is stored in the cloud.

06Children's privacy

Anglers is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information through a third-party service used by the app, contact us using the details below so we can address it.

07Data retention & security

Gameplay progress is stored only locally on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app. Analytics and advertising data collected by our third-party providers is retained according to their own policies, linked above. We rely on those providers' security practices for any data they process on our behalf.

08Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of Anglers after a change means you accept the updated policy.
