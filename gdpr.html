01Data controller

For the purposes of the GDPR, the data controller for Anglers is the independent developer operating under the name Bwana, contactable at the email below. Anglers is a one-person, privately developed project — there's no separate company involved.

Google acts as an independent controller (not our processor) for the data its own services — AdMob, Analytics, Play Billing — collect directly through the Google SDKs built into the App. Google's own privacy terms govern that processing; see Google's Privacy Policy.

03International data transfers

Google's services may process data outside the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, including in the United States. Where that happens, Google provides its own safeguards for these transfers, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses — see Google's Ads Data Processing Terms and Privacy Policy for details. We don't run our own servers, so we don't transfer data internationally ourselves beyond what these third-party services do.

04Your rights under the GDPR

If GDPR applies to you, you have the right to:

  • Access — ask what personal data is held about you.
  • Rectification — ask for inaccurate data to be corrected.
  • Erasure ("right to be forgotten") — ask for your data to be deleted.
  • Restriction — ask us to limit how your data is processed in certain cases.
  • Data portability — ask for data you provided to be given back in a portable format, where technically feasible.
  • Object — object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing that already happened lawfully before you withdrew it.

05How to exercise your rights

Because Anglers has no accounts, most of what's collected about you lives with Google, tied to your device's advertising ID or an anonymous analytics ID rather than to any identity we hold. In practice:

  • Ad personalization and analytics consent: use the in-app Privacy choices option in Settings (where shown), or your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads controls — see our Privacy Policy for exact steps.
  • Data Google holds directly: Google's own tools, such as Google's Data & Privacy controls, let you view, export, or delete data tied to your Google account or device.
  • Anything else, or if you're not sure where to start: email us at the contact below and we'll help route your request or delete what little we can access on our end.

06Lodging a complaint

If you believe your data protection rights have been violated, you can lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. The European Data Protection Board's list of national authorities will point you to the right one for your country; UK residents can contact the Information Commissioner's Office.

07Children

Anglers is not directed at children, and the age at which a child can consent to data processing under the GDPR varies by EU member state (typically between 13 and 16). We don't knowingly collect personal data from children below the applicable age; where consent from a parent or guardian would be required, that consent should be given by them rather than the child.
